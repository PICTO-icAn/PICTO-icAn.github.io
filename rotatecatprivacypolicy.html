Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026 Applies to the Rotate Cat mobile app

01Information we do not collect

Rotate Cat does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information. Your progress and local high-score ranking are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or to any third party.

02Advertising

The app shows rewarded video and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and personalize these ads, Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect information from your device, which can include:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device model and operating system version
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage and interaction data related to ads

This information is collected and processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy — not by us directly. You can review how Google handles it at policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

To opt out of personalized ads: Android Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization."

03Children's privacy

Rotate Cat is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the email below so we can remove it.

04Data security

Because Rotate Cat does not collect or store personal information on our servers, there is no personal data held by us that could be exposed in a breach. Any data processed by our advertising partner is handled under its own security practices.

05Third-party services

Rotate Cat uses one third-party service:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements. Privacy policy

06Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
